Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. The workshop runs entirely in your browser, with no account. Your pictures never leave your computer.

What do I need?

A 3D printer that prints PLA or PETG, and paper or card. The folder also needs a roller embossing machine; the hinge works by hand.

Which clearance should I use?

The paper thickness plus a few hundredths of a millimetre. The workshop sets it from the material: 0.15 mm for 80–120 g paper, up to 0.38 mm for 300 g card. More than that and fine detail will not press.

How should I print it?

Exactly as the file comes: the hinge stands with its axis vertical and needs no supports; folder plates lie flat, face up. A 0.2 mm layer and a 0.4 mm nozzle are enough.

Can I use a colour photo?

Yes. Choose Sculpture (AI): a depth model works out what is near and what is far, even white flowers on a white background. It downloads about 50 MB the first time.

How big can a stamp be?

Up to 320 × 200 mm. A 210 mm hinge prints 210 mm tall, so check your printer's height; folder plates can be printed one at a time.
